## Hello there! I’m Yash 👋

I’m an **AI master’s student at École Polytechnique (IP Paris)** working on a diverse range of topics.

At **Inria Paris (WILLOW)**, I worked with Cordelia Schmid and Shizhe Chen on pretraining 3D vision-language-action models used in robot manipulation. 

With Georgia Tech, I worked on multimodal video understanding. I’m a co-author of VideoConviction (KDD 2025, oral) and a co-first author of FinCap (ICCV 2025 workshop).

Before my master’s, I spent 3.5 years as a software engineer at Urban Company, building distributed systems and deploying ML-based pricing.
I enjoy understanding a method, implementing it, and working out what the experiments actually tell us.

My interests include world models, multimodal representation learning, LLM/VLM pre- and post-training, and diffusion and flow matching. I like implementing ideas, testing their assumptions, and understanding where they fail.

### Selected work

- **[Branch or Restart](https://github.com/sudo-YashBhardwaj/branch-or-restart)** — reinforcement learning for LLM agents, testing whether cheaper rollouts still provide useful training signals.
- **[Planner Atlas](https://github.com/sudo-YashBhardwaj/planner-atlas)** — world models for planning, studying when improvements in model metrics translate into better control.
- **[Diffusion Distillation](https://github.com/sudo-YashBhardwaj/diffusion-distillation)** — LoRA distillation for few-step image generation, with training and benchmarking code.

I mainly work with **Python, PyTorch, and Hugging Face**. I also enjoy [competitive programming](https://www.codechef.com/users/glitch201), with top-10 finishes in CodeChef Long Challenges
